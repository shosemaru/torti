Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Torti is a personal tracker for GLP-1 medications, published by Z, K.K. (Tokyo, Japan). This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version: your health data never reaches us. We have no server that stores it, because Torti doesn't have a server at all.

What stays on your device

Everything you record in Torti is stored on your iPhone:

  • Medications, doses, dates, times and injection sites
  • Weight entries
  • Side effects and notes
  • Water and protein entries
  • Progress photos
  • Vial inventory

If you have iCloud enabled, this data also syncs through your own iCloud account, which is controlled by you and covered by Apple's privacy terms. We cannot see it, and we have no ability to retrieve it.

Progress photos are not uploaded to us or to any third party. They are stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone. Like other app data on iOS, they may be included in your device backup if you have iCloud Backup turned on — that copy belongs to your Apple account, not to us. We have no way to see or retrieve it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Torti reads your weight from Apple Health so you don't have to enter it twice, and writes weights you log back to Apple Health so your other apps stay in sync. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app.

Health data is used only inside the app. It is never transmitted to us and never shared with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use Health data for marketing.

What third parties receive

RevenueCat (purchase processing and analytics)

When you start a trial or buy a subscription, RevenueCat receives the purchase event and an anonymous identifier generated by the app. It does not receive your name, email, or any health information. We use this to know how many people subscribe, so we can decide what to build next. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple (payments)

All purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

That is the complete list. Torti contains no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party analytics beyond the purchase events described above.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not require an account to use the app
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not use your data to train any model

Your control over your data

  • Export: You can export everything as a CSV at any time, for free, from Settings. This works whether or not you are a paying subscriber.
  • Delete: Deleting the app removes the data from your device. To remove synced copies, delete Torti's data from iCloud in your iPhone's Settings (Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage).
  • Withdraw permissions: Health and notification permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings at any time. The app continues to work without them.

Children

Torti is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Medical disclaimer

Torti helps you keep your own records. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The estimated medication level is a calculation based on published half-life values, not a measurement of your body. Always follow your care team's instructions.

Torti does not calculate doses. You enter the dose and the syringe units you were prescribed, and the app records them. Confirm any dosing question with your prescriber or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is handled, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
